What is End of Elysium?

End of Elysium (EoE) is a visual novel adventure game about Lilith, Elysium's worst bartender, and her conversations with the most terrible patrons she's ever met.

Unfortunately for Lilith, dealing with Elysium's unsavoury clientele requires tact, charisma, and an overdose of patience... but, for employees lacking in all three, logical deduction is management's approved alternative.

Use your logic to cut through lies, confront the past, and do whatever it takes to reach the truth—even if it burns everything Elysium stands for to the ground.

CHARACTERS

LILITH An employee of Elysium. Terrible at conversation, and an even worse bartender. ASMODEUS DEVA An employee of Elysium. Pretends to be grumpier than he really is. VESPER ASPHODEL Elysium's bar manager by necessity, not choice. Never on time for work. ROSARIO ROJAS A biker who speaks with actions instead of words. Prefers to keep the helmet on. TIBERIUS GREY A wisecracker with a resigned personality. Perpetually on the verge of sleep. JERICHO MOON A charismatic man with an eye for detail. Never forgets to wear his smile. CYNTHIA BELMONTE A reserved young lady who blends into the background. Dislikes eye contact. RAMESES LAURENT A famous actor at the height of his career. Never gets tired of saying his own name. KIRARI AMAMI An enigmatic woman at the whims of her own curiosity. Prone to staring. ISADORA HAWTHORNE A showwoman with a silver tongue. Took the phrase "rose-coloured glasses" literally. VALENTINE ZHURONG A restless, reckless woman quick to panic. Wears her heart on her sleeve against her will. NAZARETH ALDERAMIN An aloof gossip with an attitude. Very particular about appearances. DAMIAN QUINN A smug man who never hears "no". Loves flaunting his tattoos and his knife, in either order. EMERY IRIS An antisocial grifter. Has no qualms about lying through their pointed teeth. MAGNOLIA BELMONTE A calculating businesswoman and mother. Difficult at her best; impossible at her worst.
