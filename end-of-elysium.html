ABOUT

End of Elysium (EoE) is a visual novel adventure game being developed by evelili.

The game follows Lilith, a woman who has no business being a bartender, and her conversations with the patrons of Elysium. However, each patron has their own unsavoury reasons for coming to her establishment.

If Lilith wants to fully uncover their stories, she must use logic to connect conversations and find the hidden truth of her patrons' lives.

CHARACTERS

LILITH An employee of Elysium. Terrible at conversation, and an even worse bartender. ASMODEUS DEVA An employee of Elysium. Pretends to be grumpier than he really is. VESPER ASPHODEL Elysium's bar manager by necessity, not choice. Never on time for work. ROSARIO ROJAS A biker who speaks with actions instead of words. Prefers to keep the helmet on. TIBERIUS GREY A wisecracker with a resigned personality. Perpetually on the verge of sleep. JERICHO MOON A charismatic man with an eye for detail. Never forgets to wear his smile. CYNTHIA BELMONTE A reserved young lady who blends into the background. Dislikes eye contact. RAMESES LAURENT A famous actor at the height of his career. Never gets tired of saying his own name. KIRARI AMAMI An enigmatic woman at the whims of her own curiosity. Prone to staring. ISADORA HAWTHORNE A showwoman with a silver tongue. Took the phrase "rose-coloured glasses" literally. VALENTINE ZHURONG A restless, reckless woman quick to panic. Wears her heart on her sleeve. NAZARETH ALDERAMIN A stylish man with an aloof attitude. Very particular about appearances. DAMIAN QUINN A smug man who never hears "no". Loves flaunting his tattoos and his knife, in either order. EMERY IRIS An antisocial grifter. Has no qualms about lying through their pointed teeth. MAGNOLIA BELMONTE A calculating businesswoman and mother. Difficult at her best; impossible at her worst.
